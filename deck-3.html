Diagnosis / where margin leaves the business

Four leaks. Three of them are mine to close.

01 · fitment

Wrong-part returns

On a VIN-specific catalogue, most returns aren't faults. They're a compatibility promise the page didn't keep.

02 · routing

Shipping from the wrong hub

Two stock locations means every order has a right origin. Wrong one costs duty, freight and days.

03 · RMA

Returns that were never faults

Coding and install error look like defects on a form. Working modules travel twice.

04 · cover

Long-lead SKUs out of stock

High-ticket items with the longest lead times. Every week unavailable is expensive twice.

I'd confirm each against your data in week one — but I'd bet on returns being the biggest, because it usually is.
